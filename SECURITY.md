# Security policy

ZELTA is an early-stage personal finance project. It is not yet handling real bank data. If you believe you
have found a security problem, please tell us privately first.

## Reporting
* Email: **security@[your-domain-here]** (replace this before publishing; until a real address exists, contact the
  maintainer through GitHub: @Tioluwanim). Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.
* Include: what you found, steps to reproduce, the impact, and any suggested fix.
* We aim to acknowledge within 5 working days and to tell you when it is fixed.

## Good-faith research
We will not pursue legal action against research that: stays within this policy, uses only your own accounts or
synthetic data, does not access, change or keep other people's data, does not degrade the service (no DoS, no spam),
and gives us reasonable time to fix before disclosure (90 days by default).
Out of scope: social engineering of people, physical attacks, third-party services (Firebase, Google Cloud, Rumpty,
Mono, Bayse), and testing against production with real users' data.

## For authorised testers
If we invited you to test, follow the written rules of engagement we sent you (`zelta_ai/docs/PENTEST.md`).

## No bounty yet
There is no paid reward programme. We will credit you if you wish.
